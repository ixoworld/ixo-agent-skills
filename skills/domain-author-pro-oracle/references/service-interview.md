# Professional-service interview

Record established facts, proposed practices, and unresolved inputs separately. Keep source instruments
and the expert's words so later revisions can explain what changed.

## Mandate and accountability

Identify the service operator, commissioning party, affected parties, and the authority that adopts the
constitution. Ask which facts or assessments the service provides, which protocols it supports, and which
work it excludes. Separate the service entity, runtime account, and endpoint signing identity.

For an existing oracle, read its current governing package and amendment procedure before drafting changes.
For a new oracle, record founding-authority evidence as unresolved until independently verified.

## Methods and evidence

Ask which evidence sources, instruments, methods, and professional standards the expert already uses.
Identify provenance, recency, uncertainty, conflicting evidence, and limitations. Record how the service
handles insufficient evidence today. Do not replace existing engine outcomes or review rules.

For a fact-producing service, map methods to the engine manifest's requested fact names. For QiForge,
describe permitted assistance and existing tool boundaries. An advisory observation can flag a mismatch;
it cannot change the commissioner's rubric or create tool authority.

## Responsibilities and recourse

Document conflicts, recusal, confidentiality, data retention, human review, complaints, appeals, and incident
response. Record who owns each practice. Distinguish an existing obligation from a proposed improvement.
Do not invent legal duties or accreditation from the word professional.

## Governance and learning

Determine who may adopt, interpret, amend, suspend, and restore the service's documents. Independent review
is conditional on the applicable procedure. Constitutional adoption and reviewing a customer's claim are
different operations with different authority.

Ask what the expert wants to learn from observations. Record uncertain rules and missing checks without
converting them into blockers. Review findings with the expert before proposing an amendment. Keep all
prior revisions and observations. Do not rewrite documents automatically from model feedback.

## Launch handoff

Capture the intended network, deployment targets, hosting account reference, service offer, and existing
commercial setup. Start on testnet. Leave credentials in the sign-in and hosting tools. Link pending
deployment work to the authoring session instead of asking the expert to repeat the interview.
