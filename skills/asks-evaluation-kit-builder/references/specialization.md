# Optimize the evaluation for the Ask

Reuse the mechanics, not the domain verdict. Start with the consequence of a wrong acceptance and the evidence available within the Ask's latency, cost and privacy constraints. If those constraints cannot support the promised judgment, narrow the deliverable or make review explicit rather than lowering a hidden threshold.

## Choose a verification strategy

| Work being requested | Useful evaluation unit | Prefer | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market intelligence | Atomic assertion and decision-relevant research report | Primary sources, dissent, period/unit reconciliation, source lineage and scoped risk review | Correct trading prediction from good report quality |
| Software or design deliverable | Agreed artifact and observable acceptance behavior | Pinned artifact, reproducible environment, functional/accessibility checks as requested | Functionality from attractive screenshots or prose |
| Field measurement | Observation at a defined place/time with instrument context | Chain of custody, instrument calibration, uncertainty and corroboration appropriate to the domain | Physical truth from a signed upload or geotag alone |
| Forecast | Precisely defined event, probability and resolution | Ex ante horizon, resolver, void rule, baseline; later proper scoring | Forecast skill from one outcome or acceptance before resolution |

The last three rows are specialization examples, not claims of validated adapters in this repository.

## Preserve configurable participation

The same evaluation can support named respondents, a network Call, or an open Ask, but participation proofs differ. Specify who controls selection, who can respond, and how eligibility is checked. A signed selection can prove that an authorized asker selected a respondent for this exact brief. It does not certify competence. A credential can establish a scoped qualification if its issuer and status are trusted; it does not certify this completion's correctness. Self-hosted verifier agents need scoped authorization and the same output/binding checks as hosted agents.

Do not demand a professional issuer before deciding whether professional qualification is part of the Ask. Conversely, do not substitute signed selection when the agreed brief requires a credential. Changing participation terms requires explicit versioned agreement, as happened in the NVIDIA pilot.

## Derive the rubric

For each criterion, state an observable condition, allowable evidence, pass/fail anchors, uncertainty and applicability. Use PARTIAL only when it has a defined meaning. N/A requires a brief-authorized scope rule; it must not erase a hard requirement. Specify how exclusions affect a score denominator. Favor a decision without a numeric score if a score adds no useful information.

Integrity, rights and other use-case hard gates cannot be offset by polished prose or higher scores elsewhere. An excellent defect report may describe a defective product; evaluate the requested report independently from the product's quality. Similarly, bearish research can be excellent research.

Model-generated suspicions require evidence and appropriate adjudication. Distinguish an observed contradiction, an uncertain hypothesis, a confirmed deterministic violation and an authorized human decision. Keep domain risk (for example revenue concentration) separate from conduct or processing-policy risk.

## Minimize cost without losing evidence

Use deterministic checks for hashes, schemas, arithmetic, expiry, authorization and exact locators. Use models for bounded interpretation where necessary; use humans for required judgment or unresolved ambiguity. Send only permitted relevant material, retaining links to exact originals. Deduplicate repeated transport content without changing the bound logical packet. Predeclare sampling, token budgets and time limits; truncation is a failed input preparation step, not permission to omit difficult evidence silently.

An optimization must preserve required evidence coverage, provenance and policy meaning. Measure latency/cost alongside error and abstention rates. A cheaper judge that avoids difficult cases is not necessarily a better service.
