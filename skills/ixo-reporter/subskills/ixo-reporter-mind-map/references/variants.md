# Mind map variants

Branch names are suggestions; write them in the reader's language. Every node's label is a statement with a basis.

## overview

| Branch | Label basis | Children |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Result | `check` (cite the groups), a few words | One `check` child per group that did not pass, except evidence files |
| Achieved | `reading` citing the headline facts | One `signed` child per figure, counts included: "Participants: {F2}" |
| Who was involved | `reading` or `missing` | `signed` children only for facts that name people, roles or organisations; otherwise one `missing` child |
| How it was measured | `reading` or `missing` | Usually one `missing` child: certificates rarely record method |
| Evidence | `check` citing `evidence` | "Reporter did not open the evidence files"; no children needed |
| Not confirmed | `missing` | What the certificate does not record: evaluator, cost, attribution. Do not repeat the check groups already under Result or Evidence. |

## evidence-trail

| Branch | Label basis | Children |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Result | `check` | As above |
| The claim | `reading` citing the facts | What the certificate asserts, in one line |
| The figures behind it | `signed` | One child per fact |
| What Reporter checked | `check` | One child per group that passed, using Reporter's group titles |
| What nobody checked | `check` and `missing` | Evidence files, the truth of the statements, evaluation method |

## Depth

- `short` (2 levels): the centre and the branches only. Put the figure placeholder in the branch label: "Achieved: {F1}".
- `default` (3 levels): branches with children, as in the tables.
- `long` (4 levels): children may have one more level, for example a figure and what it is as at.
